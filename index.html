An addon for World of Warcraft: Forever

Chronicle

Your character's story, written as you play.

Everything you would look up on a website, Chronicle learns as you go: who has work for you, where a foe lives, what it drops, who was with you in the Deadmines. You build your own book of the world, and it only knows what your character has seen.

An evening in the Timeline

Chronicle is one window with a tab for each part of your records. The Timeline is the first: each day you play, told in your character's words.

Every highlighted name leads somewhere: a place to the Atlas, the Deadmines to its page in Dungeons, a companion to everything you have done together.

Hints that grow as you do

When your character is ready for a dungeon or the next part of their class training, the Journey opens a chapter for it. Its hint starts vague and fills in as you explore and ask around, so you find the work yourself instead of reading a guide.

A database you build by playing

Chronicle writes down what you kill, loot, skin, gather, make, trade and finish, and each tab reads from the others. It knows only what you have done, so every number in it comes from your own play.

Journey

A chapter for every class quest chain and every dungeon, with hints from innkeepers and trainers. Each step you finish is its story, retold in your character's words.

More of the same

  • Search by what drops. Type a thing's name in the Atlas and it lists the foes that dropped it, zone by zone. In Professions, the same search lists every recipe that takes it.
  • Who was there. Search Companions for a dungeon, a zone or a foe to see who was with you.
  • What killed you. A death names the foe and leads to its Bestiary page. Swear vengeance on it, and the Bestiary keeps count of how many you have killed since.
  • Dungeon bosses. Each boss you killed has a Bestiary page with what it dropped for you, and a dungeon's quests lead to its chapter in the Journey.
  • Trades. What changed hands, any money, and with whom. A companion's name leads to their page.
  • New recipes. A recipe learned at a trainer leads from its Timeline entry straight to the recipe in Professions.

The Timeline and Deeds are linked the same way, and every tab has a search box. The screens on this page are drawn from the addon's own layout and words; in the game they wear its parchment art and the game's own icons.

What sets it apart

  • Your race writes it. A dwarf sounds like a dwarf and a troll like a troll, for every race and class, Skyborne included.
  • It never changes how you play. No quest arrows, no rotation helpers, no nagging. It writes down what you did.
  • No spoilers. It only knows what your character has seen or been told.
  • Easy on your game. Old days fold into months, so your records stay small and quick.
