As you play

When something worth remembering happens, a slim notice slides in near the top of your screen. A quest step fills its ring as you make progress.

Hover to read more. Point at a notice for the whole story behind it.
Click to go there. A new foe opens in the Bestiary, a place in the Atlas, a quest on your map.
Make it yours. Drag the notices wherever suits your screen, and turn off any kind you would rather not see.

What sets it apart

Your race writes it

Your adventures are told in your character's own voice, and your race colors how it is said: a dwarf sounds like a dwarf, a troll like a troll.

It never changes how you play

No quest arrows, no helpers, no reminders nagging you. It watches what you do and writes it down; the game stays the same game.

It stays out of the way

Each notice is one slim line that fades on its own. Drag them wherever you like, and choose which kinds you want to hear about.

A new level is an occasion

Reaching a new level gets a real celebration: your class crest, the game's own fanfare, and the new skills you can train.

No spoilers

Chronicle shows only what your character has seen or been told. Hints grow as you learn more, and nothing ahead of you is given away.

Easy on your game

It keeps the moments that matter and lets the everyday ones go, so its saved records stay small.

Everything links

Every highlighted name leads to its page in another tab.
