An addon for World of Warcraft: Forever

Chronicle

Your character's story, written as you play.

Everything you would look up on a website, Chronicle learns as you go: who has work for you, where a foe lives, what it drops, who was with you in the Deadmines. You build your own book of the world, and it only knows what your character has seen.

Hints that grow as you do

When your character is ready for a dungeon or the next part of their class training, the Journey opens a chapter for it. Its hint starts vague and fills in as you explore and ask around, so you find the work yourself instead of reading a guide.

Four tabs that feed each other

Journey

A chapter for every class quest chain and every dungeon, with hints from innkeepers and trainers. Each step you finish is its story, retold in your character's words.

Places to the AtlasDungeons to their page

Atlas

Maps that fill in as you explore. Each zone keeps the foes you fought there, what you gathered and the quests you did. Click a foe to see on the map where you fought it, and set a waypoint.

Foes to the BestiaryHerbs and ore to Professions

Bestiary

Every foe you have killed: what your race and class make of it, what it dropped, and where you met it. Click a drop to see which other foes have dropped it.

Where you fought it, in the AtlasDrops to a search

Dungeons

Every run and clear: who came, how long it took, which bosses fell and what they can drop. A run that fell apart is remembered too.

CompanionsIts quests in the Journey

The Timeline, Professions, Companions and Deeds are woven in the same way. Every tab has a search box.

What sets it apart

  • Your race writes it. A dwarf sounds like a dwarf and a troll like a troll, for every race and class, Skyborne included.
  • It never changes how you play. No quest arrows, no rotation helpers, no nagging. It writes down what you did.
  • No spoilers. It only knows what your character has seen or been told.
  • Easy on your game. Old days fold into months, so your records stay small and quick.
