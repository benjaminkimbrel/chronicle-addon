Patch Notes

What changed in each version, newest first.

Newest · November 10, 2026

1.4.217

Everything since 1.4.190.

New

  • The Overview: a new first tab.
    • Latest: today’s four figures (levels gained, foes killed, quests done, the purse), then the day’s biggest moment in your character’s own words, then the moments before it. Click any of them to open its page.
    • Up next: your class quests, the abilities ready to train (or what your next level brings), the three dungeons nearest your level you haven’t cleared, and your professions. Each profession’s bar is marked where its next rank can be trained.
  • Quest progress as you go: each quest’s steps show in one notification that updates in place, its ring filling as you go: “2 of 8 Kobold Vermin slain for Investigate Echo Ridge.” When everything’s done, it tells you the quest is ready to hand in. The game’s own yellow quest messages are held back while these are on. Click one to track the quest and open the map at it.
  • Notifications fill their ring: a notification with progress fills its ring around the icon. Quest hand-ins, dungeon clears, trophies, and kills of rares and bosses sweep the whole ring with a burst, a pulse and a sheen.
  • Foes in your character’s words: a new foe, a rare or an elite now shows its description on a card under its notification, the way places do.
  • Let it go: let go of a sworn foe from its Bestiary page. The players you’ve sworn vengeance on are listed in Companions, under Sworn enemies.

Changes

  • The Timeline is retired. The Overview takes its place, and Chronicle no longer keeps routine records: quests taken and ordinary quests handed in, merchants, hearths, flights, trades, parties and the like. The first time you log in on this version, those old records are cleared out once, which makes your saved files much smaller. Everything of note stays.
  • Quest notifications name their quests: “You took on Book Collector in Elwynn Forest.” Two at once are both named; three or more are counted.
  • Clicking a notification goes somewhere useful: a quest you took on opens the map; a class or dungeon quest you finished opens the Journey; a death opens the foe in the Bestiary. The rest simply close.
  • Professions are listed under Primary and Secondary.
  • Level-ups no longer hold other notifications back.
  • Names in the words lead where you’d expect: a boss opens in the Bestiary, and a dungeon’s name opens that run in the Journey.
  • The Journey’s first row is now called Summary, and Settings shows Chronicle’s version at the bottom left.

Fixes

  • A dungeon run left open overnight now ends when you log in.
  • Moonkin are listed with the owlbeasts, not the night elves.
  • Hunters: Chronicle no longer changes what Beast Training’s window shows.

To report a bug, type /chronicle bug in game; for an idea, /chronicle suggest.

Earlier versions

1.4.190

November 10, 2026

Everything since 1.4.189.

New

  • Chronicle says when it’s been updated: after you reload or log in on a new version, a notification tells you which version you’re on now.
  • A heads-up from the people you play with: when someone in your group or guild has a newer Chronicle than yours, a notification tells you once, so you know to update. You can turn both off in Settings, under “Notify me of”, “Chronicle itself”.
  • Chronicle Updater can check by itself: tick “Check for updates automatically” and it checks when you sign in to Windows and every four hours. When there’s a new version, it puts it in place and leaves a Windows notification. Nothing runs in between, and you can untick it any time.

Changes

  • Chronicle Updater: checks for a new version first, so when you’re already up to date it says so at once without downloading anything. The patch notes have more room.

To report a bug, type /chronicle bug in game; for an idea, /chronicle suggest.

1.4.189

November 10, 2026

Everything since 1.4.185.

New

  • An updater: Chronicle Updater finds your game, downloads the newest Chronicle and puts it in place, with the patch notes beside it. Run it whenever you like, even with the game open, then type /reload.

Changes

  • Rogue quests in the Journey: each race’s way into Redridge, Redridge Rendezvous and Alther’s Mill are now one chapter, instead of separate pieces with the step you’re on missing. The Horde’s chain from the Salute to Hinott is one chapter too.
  • Warrior armor quests at 20: on the Alliance, everything from Yorus Barleybrew to your own race’s smith and armor is one chapter (the night elves’ and dwarves’ had been missing). On the Horde, each Brutal piece is a chapter of its own, open to every warrior after Brutal Armor.
  • Journey stories read better: about 600 quest stories that all began “So-and-so told me…” now open in many different ways, so a chain no longer reads the same step after step.

To report a bug, type /chronicle bug in game; for an idea, /chronicle suggest.

1.4.185

November 9, 2026

Everything since 1.4.151.

New

  • Places found have a new notification: a pill (“You came into Westfall.”) with your character’s own words for the place on a card beneath it. The card fades in with the sound of a quill, its lines balanced to an even length, and it leaves with its pill once you’ve had time to read. In the light theme the card is brown leather; in the dark theme it is dark, like its pill.
  • Your character’s voice everywhere: the Atlas and the Bestiary now say what your Timeline says, in your character’s own words, in their pages and their notifications.
  • Hover a notification for the whole entry: every notification with a Timeline entry shows that entry’s words when you point at it.
  • Test a place: under Settings, Notifications, “Show a test place” shows a place’s pill and card, short, medium and long in turn.

Changes

  • Notifications over the game’s messages: they now appear above the red error messages and quest progress lines instead of behind them.
  • No doubled discovery line: while Chronicle shows a place you found, the game’s own yellow “Discovered” line is held back, so the two don’t overlap.
  • Stacked notifications wait below: the ones waiting always sit just under the one in front, and step up into place as it leaves.
  • Quest notifications say where a quest is from: “You finished a task from Westfall.” when you hand it in somewhere else, and “You gave up a task from Westfall.”
  • Quest tooltips: a quest’s description is in light gray under its title.
  • Rumors at innkeepers and trainers: never cut short, however long, and no longer shift as they appear.
  • Settings tidied: “Card position” and “Pills with the cards” are gone (the pills stand on their own), and the descriptions on the Notifications page match how things work now. Card size starts at Small, and sizes a place’s card and the level-up’s windows.

Some of these came in through the forms. Thank you! To report a bug, type /chronicle bug in game; for an idea, /chronicle suggest.

1.4.151

November 9, 2026

Everything since 1.4.148.

New

  • Levels stay in the Timeline with Level-Up off: switching the Level-Up module off used to stop the “Level N” entries and the entries for what your trainer taught you. Now they’re written either way. Switching it off turns off only the celebration and its notifications.
  • Every new ability on a level’s entry: a level’s entry names every ability your trainer has for you at that level, not three and “more”.
  • Rumors look like the notifications: the rumor buttons beside an innkeeper’s or class trainer’s conversation are pills now. They follow your theme and pill size.

Fixes

  • A quest you give up keeps its own zone: giving up a quest in a different zone from where you picked it up showed it under the zone you were standing in. Its entry and notification show the quest’s own zone now.

The level changes and the quest fix came in through the forms. Thank you! To report a bug, type /chronicle bug in game; for an idea, /chronicle suggest.

1.4.148

November 8, 2026

Everything since 1.4.146.

Fixes

  • Bestiary groups close with a foe selected: clicking a group to close it while one of its foes was selected opened it again at once. Now it closes, and the foe is deselected.
  • Crafting no longer pushes the game’s windows aside: after crafting from the Professions tab, the character window (and others) opened toward the middle of the screen. They open in their usual place now.

Both came in through the bug form. Thank you! To report another, type /chronicle bug in game.

1.4.146

November 8, 2026

Everything since 1.4.143.

Report a bug, suggest something

  • /chronicle bug opens a box with a few details already selected: your Chronicle version, your race, class and level, where you are, and any errors Chronicle has kept. Press Ctrl+C to copy them. Then open the bug form in your browser, say what happened, and paste them in. The form’s link is in the box too.
  • /chronicle suggest shows the link to the suggestions form.
  • Neither form asks you to sign in or for your email.

Fixes

  • A friendly NPC isn’t a foe: an escort who died beside you (Volcor in Darkshore) was written in the Bestiary. Now nothing friendly is, and one already there leaves the Bestiary the next time you target it.
  • The right picture on a pill: a pill could show the last pill’s picture (a party member out of sight showed a bag). It shows their class icon now.
  • Journey pills open at their quest: “View in Journey” on a pill selects the quest in the list, so the page beside it isn’t empty.

1.4.143

November 8, 2026

Everything since 1.4.141.

Pills

  • Pills stack by default now: one at a time, with the next ones peeking out behind. Everyone is switched to this once. To see them all at once, untick “Stack the pills” under Settings → Notifications.
  • Starting a class quest chain shows one pill (“… begins a new chapter of your Journey.”), not a second “You took on …” beside it.
  • A pill whose entry has left the Timeline (a quest taken and dropped right away) just goes away when you click it.

1.4.141

November 8, 2026

Everything since 1.4.132.

The level-up, remade

  • A new banner at the top of the screen. Your class crest sits in a round seal, darkened, showing the level you had. Then the number steps up to the new one with WoW’s own level-up sound and a chime. The seal pulses, a sheen sweeps across it, it lights up from inside, and soft rays of light turn behind it. Then a pill unrolls beneath it, saying your character reached the new level.
  • It stays for the whole celebration and leaves with the two windows.
  • The Attributes window opens at your new level, since the banner has already counted it up. Each attribute and skill lights up with the same highlight as the lists in the Chronicle window.

Choose your notifications

  • A new page in Settings, “Notify me of”, lists every kind of notification by group: Foes, Deaths, Quests, Journey, Dungeons, Professions, Places and travel, Merchants, Companions, Trophies and milestones, Class skills and Level-ups.
  • Untick a group to hide all of it, or untick single kinds inside it. Unticking a group hides its list too, and each kind keeps its own setting for when you tick the group again.
  • The old “New foe pills” and “New skill pills” switches are part of this list now. If you’d turned either off, it stays off.

More pills

  • New pills for things that were only in the Timeline: quests you finish, take on or give up (“You finished a task in Westfall.”), a dungeon run that ended before its last boss, a trade, things you made that you sold or gave to another player, and milestones.
  • Several at once join up (“You took on three tasks.”).
  • Clicking any pill that has no page of its own, such as a trophy or new skills, opens its entry in the Timeline.
  • No more count badge at the end of a pill; the sentence already says how many.
  • Pills sit a little higher by default, clear of the quest progress messages at the top of the screen. “Pill position: Reset” puts them there.

Dungeons

  • “Other foes”: good loot from a dungeon’s ordinary foes (blue quality or better) now has its own row after the bosses, on the dungeon’s page in the Journey and in the Bestiary. It shows how many of your runs there saw each item.

Smaller things

  • A quest you give up now shows a red X drawn to match the other quest marks, over its zone’s picture.

1.4.132

November 8, 2026

Everything since 1.4.101.

Notifications are pills now

  • Most notices are one line. A new foe, a recipe or skill learned, something gathered for the first time, a trophy, a death, a dungeon cleared, a sale at a merchant and the rest now show as a slim one-line “pill” on the card’s own paper, with a round icon. Only a new place or capital keeps the full card, and a new level keeps its celebration.
  • Point at a pill to read more. Its tooltip has everything the old card said: a boss’s own description, the facts of a run, a hint of where to go next.
  • Click a pill to open its page in the Chronicle window, or right-click it to put it away.
  • The same kind join up. Several new foes from one pull become “Three foes were added to your Bestiary.”, with a count.
  • Two pills have a button: a death has “Swear vengeance”, and a dungeon clear in a group has “Thank the party”.
  • New notices: a new flight path, a new companion, and a dungeon entered for the first time.
  • Flights: the ticket is a pill at the top of the others with the time left. When you land it fades away and “You landed in …” comes in.

Placing and sizing them

  • Pills and cards have their own places. By default the pills sit at the top of the screen in the middle, and the cards sit just above your chat window. Drag a card or a pill to move them; dragging near the middle of the screen snaps to the center.
  • Pills off to one side line up by that edge, so they stay neat on the left or right of the screen.
  • New settings under Settings → Notifications (the page that was “Cards”):
    • “Pill size”, apart from “Card size”.
    • “Pills with the cards”, to keep them together instead.
    • “Stack the pills”: one at a time with the next ones peeking out behind (the default), or all at once.
    • “Lock in place”, so you can’t drag them by accident.
    • “Escape puts them away”: turn it off and Escape no longer dismisses cards, pills or the level-up celebration.
    • “Show a test card” and “Show a test pill”, to place and size them, plus a reset for each one’s position.
  • If you’d changed any of these settings before, they’re kept.

The Bestiary and the Journey

  • Nearly every dungeon boss has its own description and picture, from the Deadmines to Blackrock Spire, Dire Maul, Scholomance, Stratholme and Forever’s own new dungeons. (The Karazhan Crypts and Manor Mistmantle come later.)
  • The Bestiary groups undead and mechanical foes by what they are, and bosses stand alone. Totems and wards are no longer counted as foes.
  • A run’s page names the run (“Your first run”), with its date and its best loot as the picture.
  • Clicking a run in a companion’s history opens that run in the Journey and scrolls to it.

Fixes

  • Escape closing a merchant no longer swallows the notice for your sale.
  • A link to a recipe or something gathered always shows it in Professions, even if your filters would have hidden it.
  • A night elf’s line about going deep underground names Elune instead of the sky.
  • The Hall of Thanes ends with its real final boss.

1.4.101

November 8, 2026

Everything since 1.4.11.

The Journey

  • Dungeons now live in the Journey. The Dungeons tab is gone. Each dungeon has its own page in the Journey, with a header showing its picture, name and progress.
  • A dungeon’s page has a quest list, bosses and runs. Quests you can take come first, then quests you can’t take yet, then finished ones. Bosses link to their page in the Bestiary. Runs are listed by date, and each run shows the loot seen in that run and who went with you.
  • Quest chains fold into one row. The row shows a small stack behind its icon. Click it to open the chain, with a line joining each quest to the next.
  • Class chapters use the same layout. A chapter’s quests are listed in story order, joined by a line. Clicking a quest opens its details on the right.
  • Quests use WoW’s own marks: a yellow ! means you can take it, a gray ! means not yet, “...” means it’s in your quest log, and a gold check means it’s done. Each mark sits over a picture of the quest’s zone. The marks are sharper now, too.
  • Clearer quest status: a quest waiting on an earlier one says “Needs another quest first” instead of showing a level, and a chain shows its level and how many of its quests are done.
  • The overview is simpler: class and dungeons sit side by side as small tiles, each leading to its page.
  • A setting to collapse quest chains: each chain shows only its latest quest until you open it.
  • Fixes: quests you could already take showed a gray mark (they’re yellow now), dungeon breadcrumbs you never took no longer show up in their chapter, and a night elf druid is no longer sent to Dalaran.
  • The Bestiary groups foes by what they are: a zone’s Defias, Gnolls, Murlocs, Wolves and so on are each one row that opens to show them. Clans join their race (Riverpaw are Gnolls), and named foes join their own people.
  • A foe’s Bestiary description is the line its card showed, sometimes followed by a line of your race’s or class’s own.

Look and feel

  • Lists highlight the chosen row with a leather plate and light the inside of its icon. Hovering a row shows a lighter version, and both fade in and out smoothly in every tab.
  • The window header is one row. The Deeds tab is gone.
  • Progress bars are smooth and blue, and look the same in every tab.
  • A companion’s History no longer collapses.
  • In the dark theme, list rows no longer have a plate behind them all the time.
  • The Bestiary’s zones use the Atlas’s zone pictures.
  • The Bestiary and Atlas sidebars collapse by section, like the Journey’s.
  • Section headings no longer have a line under them.

Other changes

  • Hall of Thanes: its second boss is now named Magmatus, and his loot is kept.
  • Foe cards: every kind of foe has many more card lines, used in turn so they don’t repeat for a long while.
  • Professions: cards and Timeline links open the right recipe even while you’re looking at another character’s professions, the tab opens its profession properly, the craft buttons keep their hover, and a recipe you’re short materials for keeps its icon in full color.
  • Class trainer: your trainer’s filters are put back as soon as Chronicle has read the list, and a talent you pick is no longer reported as a new skill.
  • Map: the foe and gathering hotspots are blue again.
  • Kobold Diggers count as kobolds, and the Deadmines’ goblin crafters as goblins (they were filed under the Defias).
  • Dungeon entrances have their own place descriptions, the Deadmines among them.
  • Close calls: a party fight no longer triggers a false close call.
  • Sharing between your characters: a dungeon’s boss kills count every character’s again, and a dungeon only your other characters have entered shows up faded in the Journey.
  • No quest completion cards for notable quests. The Timeline still records them.
