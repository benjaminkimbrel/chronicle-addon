# Chronicle 1.4.678

Released November 24, 2026.

## New

- **A fresh journal:** every character's journal starts over once, so you get the newly written entries instead of the old ones. Nothing you've done is lost: every story you've already finished waits to be written again, newest first, one entry a day. Your first page stays as you saw it.
- **More time played, more entries:** you still get one entry a day. On top of that, every two hours you actually play unlocks one extra entry if a story is waiting. Extras don't pile up: the next two hours start counting once you've written it.
- **Stories with sound:** when you write an entry at an inn, the story now has its own music, the sounds of the place, and sound effects at its big moments. Where the game gives a boss a voice, the quote appears on a screen of its own as you hear it said.
- **Your Journey on the world map:** every Journey quest giver you know about now has a marker on the world map, showing that chapter's own icon (the dungeon's, or your class's). That covers the givers you've heard of from an innkeeper or trainer, ones you've met, and the next step of every chapter you're on.
  - On a zone map, markers sit right where the NPC stands. Click one to set a waypoint, and click again to remove it. Shift-click any marker to close the map and open that quest in your Journey.
  - Zoomed out to a continent or both continents, markers sit roughly where their quests are. Click one to zoom in.
  - The full-screen map shows them larger.
- **On the minimap and in tooltips:** nearby Journey quest givers show on your minimap, and hovering one in the world adds a "Journey" line to the game's tooltip saying what it's for.
- **Track what you want:** each Journey chapter and dungeon has a "Track on the map" box. Chapters start tracking on their own when they come into your range and stop once you outgrow or finish them, and in between, your choice stands. Settings has switches for the map, the minimap and tooltips.
- **Your own weapon:** your journal names the weapon you're really carrying, and mentions your shield only if you have one.

## The journal

- Dungeon entries are taking a break while every dungeon's story is rewritten to a new standard. Your dungeon runs are all still recorded, and their entries will be ready to write as each dungeon's new story arrives.
- Companions are described in many more ways, so two companions of the same standing no longer get the same sentences.
- Many of the lines for training, deaths, flights, your hearthstone and level-ups were rewritten in your character's voice.

## The Journey

- Waypoints now work for nearly every quest giver and next step, including Forever's own quests. Stormwind's givers are placed on Forever's newer map of the city.

## Fixes

- New notifications that arrive during a level-up now wait until its windows close, then come in order.
- The Bestiary shows the right pictures for dungeon bosses, Gelihast is named correctly, and a boss's "Fought in" counts its runs.
