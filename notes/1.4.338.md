# Chronicle 1.4.338

Released November 12, 2026.

## New

- **Chronicle's own music:** songs written for Chronicle, one for the first page, one for the reading room that follows it, and a new level-up song for every level. On a level-up, the number now steps up on the song's final hit.
- **New sounds for stats and skills:** after a level-up, each stat that rises plays a note a step higher than the one before, and a skill you learn has a sound of its own.
- **The reading room:** while the first page is up, the world goes dark around a shaft of light with dust drifting in it, colored by the time of day: morning, afternoon, a violet dusk or moonlight.
  - In the evening and at night a fire glows at the bottom of the screen, with rising embers and a quiet crackle.
  - Sound effects go quiet and the game's ambience comes up a little. Everything is set back exactly as it was when the page closes.
  - Settings' button to see the first page again shows it all.
- **Rumors have a page:** a rumor's notification now slides out the rumor itself, who may have work for you and where. It can be turned off under Notify me of.

## Changes

- Names over heads are hidden while the first page is up, and set back exactly as you had them.
- Place names read better: districts, quarters, terraces, enclaves, inns and taverns take "the" ("the Trade District", "the Lion's Pride Inn").
- **Warriors:** each race's first weapon quests, at level 10, are now a Journey chapter of their own after Defensive Stance. If you take another race's weapon quests (Marshal Haggard's in Elwynn Forest after learning your stance in Teldrassil, say), that chapter shows, and your own race's weapon is no longer offered.
- The signature on the first page dries from wet gold like the ink, with the sound of a quill, and the title card waits a moment for the music.

## Fixes

- A flight cut short ("stop at the next flight point") now says where you really landed.
- The thin lines along the edges of the first page's dark bands are gone.

To report a bug, type `/chronicle bug` in game; for an idea, `/chronicle suggest`.
