# Chronicle 1.4.442

Released November 14, 2026.

## Changes

- **A new look, all Chronicle's own:** the window, tabs, buttons, lists, scroll bars, check boxes, search fields, dropdowns, tooltips, progress bars and quest marks are all redrawn in Chronicle's own painted art, in both the light and dark themes.
  - The window is a little wider, and it never runs past the edge of your screen.
  - Buttons shine as your mouse passes over them, and lists, pills and buttons light up and fade smoothly.
  - The theme setting is now simply Light or Dark. If you never changed it, Chronicle keeps the look you were already seeing.
- **Your book is now your journal:**
  - The reading room has your journal's contents: every entry by month. Click one to read it.
  - Each entry shows your level, race, class and where you wrote it, and the room takes on the hour you wrote it, with its light and its fire.
  - The links at the top of an entry name the earlier and later entries they lead to.
  - Hover over Your journal on the Overview to see how it fills and where to write in it. You can write a new entry once a day.
  - A click skips the title when you first open the reading room.
- **New music:** a new intro song and a new reading room song, with echoes of familiar melodies.
- **Notifications:**
  - A notification no longer pops up a tooltip. Its details are on its page instead, and several joined into one name each of them there.
  - Chapters opened, Dungeons opened and Something to write show their details on that page too.
  - Places are named the way the rest of Chronicle names them: "You are in the Trade District."
- **The level-up:** the blue ring around the seal fills smoothly to the new level and bursts with light when it is full. The windows that open with it have a round picture in their header.
- **Rumors and waypoints:**
  - Rumors name 53 more towns, and clicking a rumor sets a waypoint where the quest giver stands.
  - A quest's details in the Journey have a Set a waypoint button, which opens the world map at its zone.
- **Dungeons with no quests for you** are listed in the Journey once you are close to their level, marked "Not entered yet," and a cleared one says Completed.

## Fixes

- **How dwarves say "of them":** at the end of a sentence or before a pause, a dwarf now says "of 'em" ("I'm goin' to be one of 'em."), and "o' them" when the sentence runs on ("None o' them has killed me yet."). A first journal entry written before this update reads the same way.
- A foe's Bestiary note no longer runs two descriptions together: when your race's or class's own words about a foe already say what it is, they stand on their own.
- The Baron in the Ruins of Lordaeron is named The Baron, not "The Butcher," including in your old records.
- Friend of the Kirin Tor and Shrewd Negotiations now count toward the City of Dalaran, not the Ruins of Lordaeron.
- After you wrote in your journal, its row on the Overview could turn dark gray on the dark theme. It now keeps its color.
- The Journey no longer shows a stray scroll bar beside lists that don't need one.

## The Chronicle Updater

- The updater has a new look in Chronicle's own art, and it updates itself the next time you run it.

To report a bug, type `/chronicle bug` in game; for an idea, `/chronicle suggest`.
