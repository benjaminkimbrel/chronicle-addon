# Chronicle 1.4.524

Released November 17, 2026.

## New

- **Asides in your journal's dungeon stories:** when your journal retells a dungeon, your character now goes off on a tangent or two along the way, with their own opinion of what happened. What they think depends on their race and class, so a dwarf paladin and a troll shaman see the same dungeon very differently. Every dungeon has them.
- **Reflections:** after a story from your Journey, your journal adds a short paragraph in your character's own words about what has happened since. It might be how often a hunter's pet or a warlock's demon goes in first, whether the weapon a chapter gave you is still in hand, how much use a power you learned has seen, or your first clear of a dungeon and who has kept you company since.
  - Going back into a dungeon is remembered against your first visit: whether you saw it through this time, got further, went quicker, or came with the same company.
- **Five more dungeons in the Journey:** the Drowned City, Krol'dok Stronghold, Alcaz Prison, Blackmaw Hold and the Shaper's Terrace, with their quests, their stories, the game's recommended levels and a picture of their own.

## Changes

- **The Journey:**
  - Several class chapters follow the game more closely: Call of Fire is a step of the Skyborne shaman's chapter, the Forsaken paladin's chain from Fenris Keep to Old Fire-Eye is one chapter, the Brutal pieces follow Brutal Hauberk, The Syndicate Sting follows Ain't No Telling, Furen's Armor joins the Alliance warriors' armor chain, and Rediscovering the Light is a chapter of its own.
  - Many dungeon quests that lead into one another are linked now (in Blackrock Depths, Uldaman and Gnomeregan above all), so a quest's hint no longer shows before the one that leads to it is done.
  - The hints that tell you whom to speak to next were redone, and more of them name the giver's town.
  - Fifteen chapters that teach a spell (Summon Warhorse and fourteen of the priest's) show the spell's own picture, and the two versions of Desperate Prayer and of Divine Grace are versions of one chapter again.
- **Words:**
  - "Worgen", "tauren", "centaur", "kodo", "quilboar" and the like stay the same when there are more than one, and more than one succubus are "succubi".
  - A dwarf keeps "little" where it means "not much" ("little else", "think little of"), and saves "wee" for things that are small.
  - A few lines were reworded to read more naturally ("meant for someone").
- **Settings:** "Show trivial dungeons" says what it changes: Up next on the Overview, and your Journey's Summary.

## Fixes

- Vagash, Frosthowl and the other named wendigos of Dun Morogh are filed as yetis in the Bestiary, along with about 30 other named foes whose names don't say what they are. A humanoid with no notes of its own is named by its people.

To report a bug, type `/chronicle bug` in game; for an idea, `/chronicle suggest`.
