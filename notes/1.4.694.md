# Chronicle 1.4.694

Released November 24, 2026.

## The Journey on your map

- **New look:** map markers are now diamonds, like the game's own waypoints, with a cream border so they stand out on any ground. The quest's icon inside is sharper, and a marker lights up when you hover over it.
- **Clicking works like the game's map:** click a marker to close the map and open that quest in your Journey. Ctrl-click sets a waypoint, and ctrl-click again removes it.
- **On the minimap:**
  - Markers are a little larger.
  - They stay right on their NPC as you move and turn.
  - Clicking one opens its quest in your Journey, and its tooltip follows your cursor.
  - The game's own tooltip for that NPC no longer flickers up as you move off a marker.
- **The NPC's tooltip:** the Journey line shows its icon in the same diamond, with a little more room before the words.
- **Spread out:** on the world map, a dungeon's quest givers share one marker only when they're in the same zone.
- A dungeon's page has its "Track on the map" and "Show all character data" boxes side by side.

## Fixes

- Chronicle's memory no longer climbs while you play. With map markers on, it could grow by several megabytes a minute, more while moving. It now stays nearly flat, standing still or on the move.
- Chronicle uses less memory overall: each character drops the story sounds it can never hear, such as those for other races and the other faction.
