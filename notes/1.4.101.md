# Chronicle 1.4.101

Released November 8, 2026.

Everything since 1.4.11.

## The Journey

- **Dungeons now live in the Journey.** The Dungeons tab is gone. Each dungeon has its own page in the Journey, with a header showing its picture, name and progress.
- **A dungeon's page has a quest list, bosses and runs.** Quests you can take come first, then quests you can't take yet, then finished ones. Bosses link to their page in the Bestiary. Runs are listed by date, and each run shows the loot seen in that run and who went with you.
- **Quest chains fold into one row.** The row shows a small stack behind its icon. Click it to open the chain, with a line joining each quest to the next.
- **Class chapters use the same layout.** A chapter's quests are listed in story order, joined by a line. Clicking a quest opens its details on the right.
- **Quests use WoW's own marks:** a yellow ! means you can take it, a gray ! means not yet, "..." means it's in your quest log, and a gold check means it's done. Each mark sits over a picture of the quest's zone. The marks are sharper now, too.
- **Clearer quest status:** a quest waiting on an earlier one says "Needs another quest first" instead of showing a level, and a chain shows its level and how many of its quests are done.
- **The overview is simpler:** class and dungeons sit side by side as small tiles, each leading to its page.
- **A setting to collapse quest chains:** each chain shows only its latest quest until you open it.
- **Fixes:** quests you could already take showed a gray mark (they're yellow now), dungeon breadcrumbs you never took no longer show up in their chapter, and a night elf druid is no longer sent to Dalaran.

- **The Bestiary groups foes by what they are:** a zone's Defias, Gnolls, Murlocs, Wolves and so on are each one row that opens to show them. Clans join their race (Riverpaw are Gnolls), and named foes join their own people.
- **A foe's Bestiary description** is the line its card showed, sometimes followed by a line of your race's or class's own.

## Look and feel

- **Lists highlight the chosen row** with a leather plate and light the inside of its icon. Hovering a row shows a lighter version, and both fade in and out smoothly in every tab.
- **The window header is one row.** The Deeds tab is gone.
- **Progress bars are smooth and blue**, and look the same in every tab.
- **A companion's History** no longer collapses.
- **In the dark theme**, list rows no longer have a plate behind them all the time.
- **The Bestiary's zones** use the Atlas's zone pictures.
- **The Bestiary and Atlas sidebars** collapse by section, like the Journey's.
- **Section headings** no longer have a line under them.

## Other changes

- **Hall of Thanes:** its second boss is now named Magmatus, and his loot is kept.
- **Foe cards:** every kind of foe has many more card lines, used in turn so they don't repeat for a long while.
- **Professions:** cards and Timeline links open the right recipe even while you're looking at another character's professions, the tab opens its profession properly, the craft buttons keep their hover, and a recipe you're short materials for keeps its icon in full color.
- **Class trainer:** your trainer's filters are put back as soon as Chronicle has read the list, and a talent you pick is no longer reported as a new skill.
- **Map:** the foe and gathering hotspots are blue again.
- **Kobold Diggers** count as kobolds, and the Deadmines' goblin crafters as goblins (they were filed under the Defias).
- **Dungeon entrances** have their own place descriptions, the Deadmines among them.
- **Close calls:** a party fight no longer triggers a false close call.
- **Sharing between your characters:** a dungeon's boss kills count every character's again, and a dungeon only your other characters have entered shows up faded in the Journey.
- **No quest completion cards for notable quests.** The Timeline still records them.
