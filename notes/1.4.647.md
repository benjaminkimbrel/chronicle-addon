# Chronicle 1.4.647

Released November 21, 2026.

## New

- **Your journal, rewritten:** every story your journal can tell was written again, so it reads like your character's own account and not a list of what happened. Your class decides what you notice and how you go about a task, and your race decides how you tell it. A dwarf warrior and a night elf warrior no longer sound alike.
- **Storylines:** 101 of the game's quest chains, across every zone, are now stories in your journal, told chapter by chapter as you finish them: the Missing Diplomat, Sven's Revenge, Mankrik's Wife, Hogger, Stalvan and many more. A chapter can look back on one you wrote earlier, and a storyline that leads into a dungeon ends on that dungeon's own story.
- **Dungeon stories about your party:** when your journal tells a dungeon, it tells the run you and your group made: who came, the moment each of them had, how the bosses went, and any time you went down. Friends you've traveled with are spoken of as friends, and people you just met as strangers. This covers every dungeon, for both sides, from your first clear on.
- **One entry a day:** you write one journal entry a day, at an inn, and each entry is one story. Dungeons and class chapters take turns, and storylines fill in on days when neither is waiting. Chapters of a chain always come in order.
- **Story pages end on the story:** a page ends on the story's own closing line, then your signature. The paragraph of counted reflections after a story is gone, and so are the remarks about places that used to be slotted into one, so every page reads as one story from start to finish.
- **A first page for your race and class:** a new character's first page is written for their race and class together (the Forsaken keep their waking). A character Chronicle meets partway through their adventures gets a proper first page too, not a list of facts.
- **Fellowship grows from what you do together:** kills, quests, dungeon bosses and clears, and trades (more for something you made) all count, quickly at first and more slowly toward Sworn. Your companions were recounted from what Chronicle had already seen, and no one's rank went down. A companion's page shows how close the next rank is.
- **Companions in their own words:** a new fellowship rank names something you actually did together, and each companion's page tells how you met, what you've done most, a dungeon you cleared side by side, and what they bring to a fight.

## The journal

- A page is titled by its story, with the story's picture beside it, and says where it was written ("Craftsmen's Terrace, Darnassus"). A storyline's chapter is named under the title ("Sven's Revenge, Part One").
- Page titles have a new typeface, and in the dark theme each page opens with a decorated first letter.
- The reading room's text is a size smaller, and a long story turns to a second leaf by itself, split evenly.
- Lighthearted quests are told lightly. Zenn's Bidding no longer reads like a matter of life and death.
- The Deadmines' story tells how the fight with VanCleef actually goes, quotes his own words where he says them, and ends knowing the Brotherhood isn't finished.
- The Shadowy Figure follows the clues to Raven Hill properly, and ends knowing the story isn't over.
- Pages you've already written read with the new writing too. Your first page stays as you saw it.
- A quest with more than one giver names the one you actually spoke to.
- Quest steps read plainly: "Darnassus Bank done for Eyes of the Sentinels", "Spoke with Remy for The Rite of Strength."
- The Bestiary's notes and the Atlas's remarks are in your character's voice, and the Bestiary has more to say for warriors, rogues, mages, shamans and warlocks. Places are described by what kind of place they are, and the dangers there are the ones you met.

## The Journey

- **Dungeon paintings:** every dungeon's page has its painting behind it again, and Forever's nine new dungeons have paintings of their own. The light theme's drawings are fainter, so the text is easier to read.
- A dungeon with no quests for you (Shadowfang Keep for the Alliance, for one) fills its ring once you've beaten its final boss.
- Dungeons you've outgrown move under "Show N more". A quest that starts inside a dungeon no longer keeps it on your list.
- Llana's quest in Astranaar is treated as optional for Excavation Site: Wetlands, so skipping it no longer holds the chapter back.
- Class chapters are offered to exactly the races that can take them in Forever, with new ways in for troll rogues, dwarf paladins, the Horde's felhunter and the Forsaken's Touch of Weakness. An Alliance Skyborne rogue sees the Alliance's rogue chapter at 10.
- A rare boss inside a dungeon (Bruegal Ironknuckle in the Stockade) counts as a boss of your run, and his loot is filed under him.
- Typing in the Journey's search no longer clears the "new" marks.

## The window and interface

- **Instant:** hovers, clicks and tooltips now react at once, as the game's own interface does. Windows, notifications and the level-up still fade in and out.
- **Clean fades:** notifications and their buttons, the level-up badge, the innkeeper's buttons and the level-up windows fade as one piece, with no seams showing.
- The small progress bars in lists are crisp at every window size.
- The notifications, their pages and the main window have cleaner edges.
- The main tabs are never cut short in the small window.
- The Overview says "A new entry is ready to write", or "Ready to write again tomorrow" once you've written today.
- The Professions tab no longer opens your profession to read it, the trainer's list no longer flashes, and the first click on Craft or Craft all crafts.
- A spell's rank sits beside its name in tooltips, as in the game's own.
- The Atlas opens on the zone you're actually in, and no longer lists the quests done at each place.
- The Bestiary lists everything you've skinned from a foe, with how often each came.
- In the dark theme, the brush stroke behind the level-up windows' titles is red.
- "Collapse quest chains" is gone from Settings, and a few setting descriptions say what they really do now.

## Fixes

- New spells at your trainer are listed once each, and "Multiple ranks to train" shows only when more than one rank is waiting.
- A talent's later ranks, and another race's priest spells, are no longer offered by the trainer to characters who can't learn them.
- Level-up stat changes are never shown as negative.
- Pet skills to train list only what a trainer teaches.
- The game's level-up sound no longer plays over the level-up song.
- Quest notifications come with the kill that finishes a step, never change size after they appear, and no longer count something a quest hands you as a step. "Objective Complete." is no longer a notification.
- Notifications held during combat come in the right order afterward.
- At an innkeeper, "Write in your journal" is no longer missing at first or taking a rumor button's place.
- Professions are read in full the first time you look, and quest items you loot are no longer counted as gathering finds.
- Clears of the City of Dalaran now count.
- Hamhock has the right picture, and Dextren Ward is recorded in the Bestiary.
- The search box's text starts where its hint does.
- "The Wetlands", "the Deadmines" and the like read right in the middle of a sentence.
- The Cleft in Teldrassil and a few other places are described correctly.
- Many smaller fixes to words, pictures and timing.

## Performance

- The unused day-page engine is gone, so Chronicle holds about 1.6 MB less per character, and the day records it kept are cleared from your saved files.
- About 2,450 lines of unused code and six unused pictures are gone.
- Your saved files are smaller: what your characters share is packed, and doubled or stale records are dropped.
- Logging in no longer hitches while the Atlas fills in, and a lot of everyday work is lighter.

To report a bug, type `/chronicle bug` in game; for an idea, `/chronicle suggest`.
