# Chronicle 1.4.223

Released November 10, 2026.

Everything since 1.4.217.

## Changes

- **Lighter:** what was left of the retired Timeline is gone from Chronicle's code. Nothing you see changes.

## Fixes

- **Class quests in the Journey:** some class quests showed up for only one race when more can take them. Now they show for every race that can:
  - Rogues: the poisons quest at 20 (Mathias and the Defias) for every Alliance rogue, not only humans, and as one chapter: Doc Mixilpixil's two Touch of Zanzil quests are its last two steps.
  - Shamans: the quests at 58 from Orgrimmar for tauren too.
  - Priests and warriors: the quests at 50 for every race.
  - Skyborne: the druid's cat at 20 and the mage's Report to Jennea at 15.
- "A newer Chronicle is out" now only tells you about versions that have actually been released.
- A few names in Chronicle's words no longer say "Click to view the entry" when clicking them did nothing.

To report a bug, type `/chronicle bug` in game; for an idea, `/chronicle suggest`.
