# Chronicle 1.4.498

Released November 16, 2026.

## New

- **Chronicle on your action bar:** drag a profession out of the Professions tab, or a main tab's icon, onto your action bar, and Chronicle makes it a macro with the right icon, named "C: Atlas", "C: Mining" and so on. Click it to open Chronicle right there, and click it again to close it.
  - The macros go in your account's general macros, and they're made only the first time you drag something out. They can't be made in combat; the chat says why.
  - `/chronicle open <tab>` and `/chronicle open profession <name>` do the same from chat.
- **Icons on the main tabs:** each main tab wears its picture in a ring that matches the list frames, lit when you hover over it and while it's chosen.
- **Ready to train and Train soon on a profession's page:** with no recipe chosen, a profession's page in Professions shows the recipes you can train now, and those you can train within the next 10 skill points, as a grid of icons. Each tooltip says the skill it needs, and the rank too when it's past your current one. Click an icon to open that recipe in the list.
- **A recipe's page shows what it makes:** the made item's own tooltip (or the enchant's) sits under the recipe's name, above Reagents. A requirement you don't meet yet shows in the game's red.
- **The game's own clicks on items:** ctrl-click anything Chronicle shows to try it on in the dressing room, and shift-click it to link it in chat.
- **How many a recipe makes:** a recipe that makes more than one shows how many on its picture ("1-2" where it varies).
- **Pet skills to train:** a hunter's pet skills have their own section under Ready to train on the Overview, with one icon per skill (its highest rank). The tooltip says when more than one rank is waiting.
- **Every section of the Overview folds:** click a heading to fold or unfold it. Chronicle remembers which ones you folded.

## Changes

- **The level-up has a new song**, a new take on a familiar Warcraft melody.
  - The ring around the seal fills smoothly and gives one pulse on the song's final hit, where your new level steps up.
  - The game's own level-up sound is part of the song now, right on that hit, so it no longer plays over the music. With the level-up music off, the game's sound plays as the level steps up.
  - If you keep your mouse over the level-up windows, the song plays out to its end, then your zone's music comes back.
  - The Attributes and New Abilities windows that open with it have the seal's ring around their picture, and a brush stroke paints itself in behind their title as they appear.
- **The Journey's lists:**
  - A dungeon's or class chapter's picture frame fills with blue as you finish its quests.
  - Each dungeon shows the game's own recommended levels ("Levels 18 to 23"), and a class chapter its level ("Level 18").
  - Dungeons are listed in that order everywhere: the Journey, its Summary and the Overview's Up next. A dungeon you're still below is shown faded.
  - Ragefire Chasm is hidden for the Alliance and the Stockade for the Horde, as in the game's own group finder. "Show 1 more" brings it back.
  - A dungeon's page says the level its quests start from, counting only the quests you can take.
  - A dungeon counts as trivial once its hardest quest turns gray in your quest log.
  - A quest shows its own level, with the level you can take it from where they differ ("Level 31, from 24").
- **Professions:**
  - The skill-up marks on recipes are Chronicle's own now: three, two or one chevrons for a high, medium or low chance.
  - Counts on pictures sit inside the frame, smaller, with rounded corners. When you have far more of a reagent than you need, it reads "*/20".
  - The PRIMARY, SECONDARY and CLASS headings in the Professions list fold like every other list's.
- **Your journal:**
  - You can write a new entry once 16 hours have passed since the last one, or after just 10 hours once a new day has begun (at 3 AM). That works better if you play at odd hours.
  - The reading room leaves the ambient sound at your own volume setting.
  - At an innkeeper, "Write in your journal" now stands apart from the dungeon leads, set off by a gap and in the other theme's colors, so it's harder to click by mistake.
- **How your character writes:**
  - Your character no longer talks about game mechanics. A death is a defeat ("they got the better of me"), never a graveyard walk or a spirit healer, and another adventurer is beaten, never killed.
  - Each race tells things in its own way, and faith goes with class: a night elf rogue doesn't thank Elune, and a troll warlock doesn't call on the loa.
  - A few lines were reworded to read more naturally ("Dendrite Starblaze gave me two trials", "sent me to gather water").
  - A dwarf says "wee" where it fits ("a wee bit tipsy").
  - Yetis and owlbeasts are no longer called beasts in the Bestiary.
- **The Overview:** Latest's scroll bar sits on the divider between the columns, so the text no longer shifts when it appears.
- Pictures with a tooltip light their border when you hover over them, including the picture at the top of a page, and the Journey's chain steps light their ring.

## Fixes

- Training a new profession rank updates your unlearned recipes and the Ready to train list right away, without another visit to the trainer.
- A hunter's pet skills to train no longer list skills you've already learned, and they no longer show up at all before you've learned to train your pet.
- Item and spell tooltips no longer come up empty the first time you hover over them. They fill in as soon as the game has the information.
- A recipe's skill-up mark no longer goes stale when you switch between professions.
- The Journey's rows no longer keep the old theme's colors after you switch between Light and Dark.
- The chosen tab no longer looks doubled while the window fades open or shut.
- When you loot two different things for one quest at once, you get a notification for each.
- A trade of several stacks of one thing names it once, with the total ("80 light leather"). Older trades read that way too.
- A journal entry says where you really spent your time since the last one, not the zone you happen to be standing in.
- At 24, a rogue's Journey says a rogue trainer may have a letter for you, and points you to Ravenholdt Manor only once you have it.
- Dungeon chapters follow the game more closely. Highland Hides comes after Young Crocolisk Skins, optional lead-in quests no longer hold back the quests they lead to, and Chillwind Horns now has its place before Return to Tinkee.
- A dwarf no longer writes "gave me me certificate". Words like "skins" and "pumpkins" are counted right, while "kin" stays "kin".
