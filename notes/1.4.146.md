# Chronicle 1.4.146

Released November 8, 2026.

Everything since 1.4.143.

## Report a bug, suggest something

- **`/chronicle bug`** opens a box with a few details already selected: your Chronicle version, your race, class and level, where you are, and any errors Chronicle has kept. Press Ctrl+C to copy them. Then open the bug form in your browser, say what happened, and paste them in. The form's link is in the box too.
- **`/chronicle suggest`** shows the link to the suggestions form.
- Neither form asks you to sign in or for your email.

## Fixes

- **A friendly NPC isn't a foe:** an escort who died beside you (Volcor in Darkshore) was written in the Bestiary. Now nothing friendly is, and one already there leaves the Bestiary the next time you target it.
- **The right picture on a pill:** a pill could show the last pill's picture (a party member out of sight showed a bag). It shows their class icon now.
- **Journey pills open at their quest:** "View in Journey" on a pill selects the quest in the list, so the page beside it isn't empty.
