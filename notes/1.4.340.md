# Chronicle 1.4.340

Released November 12, 2026.

## New

- **Your book:** your character now keeps a book, written a page at a time.
  - When there is something worth telling, such as a class quest chain finished, a dungeon cleared or your first crossing to another continent, and it has been at least 20 hours since your last page, a notification says you have something to write.
  - Talk to an innkeeper and choose Write in your book. The page is written out in the reading room: the story of what you did, in your character's own words, then a few lines about the days since and where you are writing from, signed at the end.
  - The Overview shows your book at the top of Up next, highlighted when a page is ready to write. Click it to read your book again in the reading room, turning between your first page and every page since.
  - Your book is on for every character. It can be turned off under Settings, Writing, and its notification under Notify me of.

To report a bug, type `/chronicle bug` in game; for an idea, `/chronicle suggest`.
