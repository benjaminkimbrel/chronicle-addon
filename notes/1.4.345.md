# Chronicle 1.4.345

Released November 13, 2026.

## Changes

- **A clearer level-up song:** the level-up has a new mix of its song, and the new level steps up right on its final hit.
- **How your character talks:**
  - Humans, dwarves and trolls now shorten their words every time they can ("I'm", "we're", "they've", "I'd"), and every race can now shorten a few more kinds of words.
  - A dwarf says "o' the" but "of it", "of an" and "of each", so the next word stays easy to read, and "o' them" for "of them".
- **Your book reads more smoothly:** the lines that close a page no longer repeat each other's words, so "too much to take on alone" is never followed by "I have not always traveled alone". A few of them are worded better too.
- A few lines that said the same words twice in one sentence were reworded, and a foe that killed you more than once on one day gives that day once.

## Fixes

- A dwarf no longer writes "'twas" where "it was" follows "of".

To report a bug, type `/chronicle bug` in game; for an idea, `/chronicle suggest`.
