# Chronicle 1.4.295

Released November 11, 2026.

## New

- **A first page for every character:** the first time Chronicle meets a character, the world dims and a title card shows their name, race, class and where they are. The title rises, and the first page of their story writes itself out beneath it in wet gold ink that dries as it goes, opening with a large gold initial, and your character signs it at the end. Next comes a short tour of Chronicle's tabs. Every character you already play gets theirs once, at their next login.
  - A character starting out gets a page about their home and why they keep the book.
  - A character met partway gets a page about where they come from, what their records show so far and where they are now.
  - Escape closes it at any time, and Settings has a button to see it again.
- **Patch notes in game:** Settings has a Patch notes page, and the "Updated" notification links to it.
- **Pages under more notifications:**
  - A quest you take shows what it asks, and class and dungeon quests show their story.
  - Class skills learned, deaths, close calls, trophies, trades, dungeon clears and more show what Chronicle wrote about them.
  - Each kind can be turned off under Notify me of.
- **Merchants have something to say:** selling now shows what the merchant made of it, in your character's words. There are many more reactions, some depend on the merchant's race (goblins included), and the item sold is named when it fits.
- **Professions:** a Class section for class skills, like a rogue's Poisons.
- **Companions:** you can now look at your other characters' companions from the list above the search, read only.

## Changes

- A quest's last step now counts to the end ("8/8") instead of showing a separate "ready to hand in" notification. That one now comes only for escorts and events with nothing to count.
- Taking a quest that's already complete gives one notification, not three.
- A finished quest chain in the Journey takes the name its quests share most.
- Companions and Professions list characters by their full names.

## Fixes

- Poison recipes that were filed under Cooking are now under the Class section.

To report a bug, type `/chronicle bug` in game; for an idea, `/chronicle suggest`.
