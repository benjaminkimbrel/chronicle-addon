# Chronicle 1.4.707

Released November 25, 2026.

The last 1.4 release. Chronicle 2.0 is in the works, so 1.4 now gets fixes only.

## Faster and lighter

- **Faster logins**, most of all on characters with a lot of records.
- **Less memory:** your account's quest and recipe details are now stored packed, and the book's stories are unpacked only when a page is written.
- **A quicker Journey:** searching it, opening a quest from a notification and folding a list all respond much faster on a long record.
- **A quicker Atlas search**, especially the first letter you type after a fight.
- **"Write in your journal" appears sooner** at an innkeeper after you finish a story.
- Notifications do less work while they show, and the flight ticket updates more calmly.

## Changes

- **Settings, General:** Saved size now has a **Measure** button. It's worked out only when you ask, so the Settings window opens faster.
- **"Condense old months" is gone.** Older records are no longer squashed into month summaries. That would have started in mid-December and quietly removed your old dungeon runs from the Journey.
- A journal set aside by a reset is now kept for 30 days.

## Fixes

- The Journey's map markers light up on hover again, instead of always looking lit.
