# Chronicle 1.4.708

Released November 26, 2026.

One fix and a new Chronicle Updater.

## Chronicle Updater

- Chronicle Updater can now put several Chronicle folders in place at once. It moves all of them together, or none of them if anything goes wrong.
- Before it changes anything, it checks every file of the download. A damaged download is refused, and your Chronicle stays as it was.
- If a part of Chronicle goes missing, the updater puts it back the next time it runs.
- It only ever touches Chronicle's own folders. Your other addons are never moved or removed.
- The updater updates itself with this release, so there is nothing new to download.

## Fixes

- A party breaking up just after someone left could remove the wrong entry from your journal. It no longer does.
